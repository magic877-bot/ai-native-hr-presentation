# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

## Locked visual direction

- Use the selected “Glass Monolith” direction: asymmetrical executive-keynote composition, deep navy canvas, restrained blue/violet glow, and one architectural glass focal object.
- In the cover title, keep `AI-Native 组织：` solid white and render all title text after the colon as a moving blue-violet neon gradient. The neon treatment must remain sharp enough for projection.
- Avoid equal-card grids, decorative HUD clutter, rainbow neon, heavy particles, and glow on body copy.
- The neon title gradient must move continuously in one direction; do not use a ping-pong or reversing animation.
- The one-direction gradient must be seamless: duplicate the complete blue-violet-cyan-blue color cycle twice and animate by exactly one cycle so the iteration boundary has identical pixels.
- Use the official 人力家 logo sourced from the company-introduction PDF, not a generated or letter-based stand-in.
- On S9, AI Harness is the business action engine; Model is only a lower-level source of general reasoning capability.
- On S10, distinguish the two sides by question rather than physical location: external adaptability explains why the organization must change and where value is moving; internal effectiveness explains how the organization must reconfigure to deliver that value. Use the framing “连接外部变化，推动内部重构”.
- On S15, prefer an attention-grabbing radar silhouette and label it as a non-quantitative illustrative profile.
- On S8, distinguish the weekly report as a fragmented-data-to-analysis/output information chain from campaign building as a cross-system execution and audit task flow; never reuse one generic stage sequence for both.
- On S8, the `1–2 天 → 2 小时` comparison must use equal-width left and right metric columns with the arrow mathematically centered; supporting labels sit beneath their own metric, never between a metric and the arrow.
- On S12, the five value-chain impact cards must remain readable at presentation distance: supporting descriptions need clearly larger type and stronger contrast than the earlier compact-card treatment.
- From S13 onward, supporting labels and explanatory copy must use presentation-distance sizing and medium-gray contrast; preserve the composition before shrinking text below a readable level.
- Use S13 as the deck-wide projection-readability reference: meaningful stage headings should visually match its hierarchy, while explanatory copy should generally render around the S13 supporting-text size. Keep only navigation, numbering, source notes, and atmospheric English labels deliberately smaller.
- S14 is a qualitative six-dimensional organization-readiness map, not a radar chart: keep the six dimensions equal-weight, center HR's translation from technical change to organizational order, and never imply invented scores. Preserve S15 as the attention-grabbing talent-profile radar chart.
- S18 must show both the current recruiting value chain and a clearly labeled hypothetical reconfiguration: candidate-side Agents increase résumé over-packaging risk, while lower-cost AI-enhanced background verification can move immediately after AI résumé screening. Render the future route as a seamless, one-direction animated dashed branch; keep consent/authorization visible and avoid implying unrestricted traditional background checks.
- On S21, keep a moderate visual overlap between the three map circles—enough to express their relationship, but never enough to cover text. Keep all three map titles, questions, and conclusions fully exposed, and keep the HR core geometrically concentric with the Harness Map circle.
- S23 is the final thank-you page. Keep it deliberately sparse: show `周虽旧邦，其命维新` first as one uninterrupted line, then place `谢谢` on the next visual line. Do not add another summary, CTA, contact block, or competing decorative object.
